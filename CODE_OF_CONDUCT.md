# Code of conduct

We welcome contributors of every background and experience level. Be respectful,
patient and constructive. Critique ideas and code, give useful context, and
respect people's time and privacy.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, personal attacks, unwanted sexual
attention and sharing private information without consent are unacceptable.
These expectations apply in project issues, pull requests and discussions.

Report concerns privately to [@djsydney04](https://github.com/djsydney04) through
the contact options on the profile. Maintainers may remove content, restrict
participation or ban contributors when needed to protect the community. They
will consider reports in good faith and protect reporters' privacy.
